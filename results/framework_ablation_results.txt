================================================================================
INTERPRETATION
================================================================================

1. The undefended BadNets model provides the baseline condition.
2. Detection methods identify suspicious poisoned samples but do not modify the model, so detection alone does not reduce ASR in this ablation comparison.
3. Fine-pruning provides the recovery/mitigation effect by reducing the backdoor behavior while retaining most clean classification accuracy.
4. The 40% configured fine-pruning experiment achieved a final clean accuracy of 73.24% and ASR of 40.54%.
5. ASR was reduced by 53.63 percentage points, corresponding to a recovery rate of 56.95%.
6. Monitoring is treated as an operational security layer. Its role is access-control/model-monitoring support rather than direct classification accuracy improvement.
7. Therefore, the framework is organized as detection, recovery, and monitoring layers rather than treating every component as an independent classifier.
